> **I design intelligent systems and live a life fueled by the same curiosity, creativity, and drive that power my research.**

Beyond my professional work, I find balance and inspiration in the arts, music, sports, and exploration.

🎨 **Art & Creativity** 
I’m an avid painter and sketch artist, working across a variety of mediums. I’m constantly experimenting with new techniques, finding joy in the quiet focus of brushstrokes and pencil lines.

🎸 **Music** 
As an amateur guitarist, I enjoy weaving melodies that offer the same sense of expression and release as my visual art. Music, like painting, is a space where I recharge and reconnect.

🏃 **Active Lifestyle** 
I keep an active routine — lifting at the gym, jogging around campus, and playing tennis. Each activity challenges me in different ways, sharpening both body and mind.

🌍 **Travel & Photography** 
Travel is my ongoing passion. I chase sunsets, explore new cultures, and capture moments through my camera lens. Every trip becomes a collection of stories told in light and color.

🥾 **Nature & Adventure** 
Hiking is my way of connecting with the natural world. Trails unfold like chapters in a book, each revealing landscapes, wildlife, and quiet moments worth remembering.
